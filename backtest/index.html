Methodology & data

How fills, costs and every metric are computed

Execution

  • Signals are evaluated on each bar's close. Orders fill at the next bar's open, so a rule can never trade on information it did not have.
  • Each entry invests 100% of current equity (fractional units). Commission and slippage are charged in basis points on every entry and exit.
  • Indicators are warmed up on the history before the start date; trades only happen inside the selected range.
  • Stop loss, take profit and max bars are checked on the close and exit at the next open. After a risk exit, the same direction is not re-entered until the rule's signal changes.
  • Buy & hold buys at the close of the first bar with the same costs. A position still open on the last bar is marked to market and shown separately; it is not counted in trade statistics.

Data

  • Daily OHLCV, dividends and splits from Yahoo Finance, refreshed every weekday by a scheduled job and served from this site. Weekly and monthly bars are built from the daily bars.
  • Prices are split-adjusted. With "Reinvest dividends" on, earlier prices are scaled down on each ex-dividend date by (1 − dividend ÷ previous close), the convention behind Yahoo's adjusted close. TD counts and indicators then run on those total-return prices.
  • Index symbols (^GSPC, ^NDX, ^HSI) are not directly tradable and carry no dividends.

Metrics

CAGR
(final ÷ initial equity)1/years − 1, years = calendar days ÷ 365.25.
Sharpe
mean(bar return − risk-free) ÷ stdev(bar return) × √(bars per year). Bars per year are measured from the data (≈252 for stocks, ≈365 for crypto, 52 weekly, 12 monthly).
Sortino
Same numerator, divided by downside deviation √(mean(min(0, excess return)²)).
Max drawdown
Largest fall from a running equity peak. Calmar = CAGR ÷ |max drawdown|.
Volatility
stdev(bar return) × √(bars per year).
Win rate
Closed trades with positive P&L after costs ÷ closed trades.
Profit factor
Gross profit ÷ gross loss of closed trades.
Exposure
Share of bars with an open position.
Beta / correlation
Of strategy bar returns against buy & hold bar returns.
