How a fill is decided

No lookahead, day session, points only

Execution

  • The tape never leaves the server. This page sends settings and receives points.
  • Day session only: bar opens in 09:15–12:00 and 13:00–16:30 Hong Kong time. Auction, lunch, and the night session are dropped.
  • A flip is known when its bar closes and is filled at the next bar’s open, on that same date. The last bar of the window does not open a new trade.
  • A 5-minute flip fills at the first 1-minute open of the next 5-minute bar. The 11:55 bar’s flip fills at 13:00.
  • A clock entry uses the trend of the last completed bar at that open. It does not need a flip.
  • The first flip after the clock uses the signal bar’s time, not the fill bar’s time.
  • Opposite flip, then flat: one exit, and nothing else that day. Same-direction re-entry waits for the entry timeframe to flip back. Reverse takes the next entry-timeframe flip either way; when both timeframes match, that is this same bar.
  • Whatever is still open flattens at the last bar inside the window, at that bar’s close.

Score

Points
One index unit. Long is exit minus entry. Short is the opposite. Gross is before the round-trip cost. Net subtracts the cost once per closed trade.
Win rate and profit factor
Counted on gross points. A scratch is not a win. Profit factor is blank when there is no losing trade.
Hold
1-minute bars from the fill to the exit, plus one when the exit is that bar’s close. Lunch has no bars, so it is not counted.
Max drawdown
Marked on every 1-minute close while the run is inside the dates. The curve itself is the cumulative result at each session’s flatten, starting from zero.
Trades per day
Closed trades divided by day-session dates in the range, including dates with no trade.
